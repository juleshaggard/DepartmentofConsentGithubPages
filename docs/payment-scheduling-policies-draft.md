# Payment and Scheduling Policies Draft

Status: Draft for Jules review. Do not publish until approved.

## Cancellation Policy

Appointments may be canceled up to 24 hours before the scheduled start time.

Cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance may be rescheduled or credited
toward a future session of the same value.

Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be treated as a missed
session and may not be eligible for a refund or credit.

If Department of Consent needs to cancel or reschedule, the client may choose a
new session time or request a full refund for the affected session.

## Rescheduling Policy

Clients may reschedule up to 24 hours before the scheduled start time using the
rescheduling link in the confirmation email.

One late reschedule request may be accommodated when possible, but availability
is not guaranteed.

Repeated rescheduling may require booking a new session.

## Refund Policy

Payments for completed sessions are non-refundable.

For individual coaching sessions, refunds may be requested up to 24 hours before
the scheduled start time.

For late cancellations, missed appointments, or no-shows, payments are not
refundable unless Department of Consent chooses to make an exception.

If a session cannot take place because of a scheduling error, technical issue,
or cancellation by Department of Consent, the client may choose to reschedule or
receive a full refund for that session.

Refunds, when approved, are returned to the original payment method. Processing
times depend on Stripe and the client's bank or card issuer.

## Package Expiration Policy

The Kink Curious to Kink Confident package includes three separate online
coaching sessions.

All three sessions should be used within 90 days of the first booked session
unless a different timeline is agreed in writing.

Unused sessions are not transferable to another person without written approval.

If a client chooses not to complete the package after the first session, unused
sessions may be credited toward future coaching at Department of Consent's
discretion. Partial refunds are not guaranteed.

## Event Companion Approval Policy

Event Companion is not available for instant purchase or immediate booking.

All Event Companion requests require a consultation and approval before payment
or scheduling is confirmed.

Approval depends on the event, location, date, timing, safety, access needs,
expectations, boundaries, and whether the service is a good fit.

Department of Consent may decline an Event Companion request for any reason,
including but not limited to safety concerns, unclear expectations, unsuitable
event conditions, timing conflicts, or requests outside the service scope.

## Event Companion Travel and Location Terms

Event Companion is available only for approved events in San Francisco and
Oakland.

The listed Event Companion price includes:

- One online preparation session.
- Up to three hours of in-person support at one approved event.
- One online follow-up session.

The listed price does not include:

- Event admission or ticket costs.
- Membership fees.
- Parking.
- Rideshare, taxi, transit, tolls, or other transportation costs.
- Food, drinks, lodging, or venue charges.

Clients are responsible for their own event ticket, admission, transportation,
and participation costs.

If Jules's event admission, parking, or required transportation costs are not
covered by the event organizer, those costs must be reimbursed by the client or
approved in writing before the event.

Support beyond three in-person hours must be approved in advance. Additional
time, if available, may be billed separately.

Event Companion does not include sexual services, play, dating, security,
bodyguard services, therapy, medical care, legal advice, intoxication
management, or crisis response.

## Payment Terms

Payment is due at booking unless a different private payment arrangement has
been approved.

Bookings are not confirmed until payment is completed, except for free discovery
calls and approved inquiry or consultation calls.

For Event Companion, payment is collected only after consultation and approval.
No public checkout link should allow immediate Event Companion purchase.

## Coaching Scope Note

Department of Consent coaching is educational and practical. It is not therapy,
medical care, legal advice, crisis support, or a substitute for professional
mental health care.
