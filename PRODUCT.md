# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults exploring kink, consent, coaching, and related products. Storefront visitors need a clear, private, accessible path from browsing to a trusted hosted checkout.

## Product Purpose

Department of Consent combines beginner-focused education and coaching with a curated commerce experience. The public site should build confidence, communicate practical expertise, and let visitors browse the live Fourthwall catalog without losing Department of Consent's identity.

## Brand Personality

Warm, candid, and deliberately provocative. The voice is practical rather than clinical, welcoming rather than performative, and confident without pressure.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic ecommerce templates, promotional widgets, badge clutter, decorative shadows, icon-heavy cards, anonymous luxury styling, and designs that make kink feel clinical, seedy, or inaccessible. Preserve the existing Department of Consent system and use the former shop only as a merchandising reference.

## Design Principles

- Make consent, clarity, and choice visible in every interaction.
- Use real people and real products as the primary visual language.
- Keep commerce restrained so the imagery and objects carry the page.
- Maintain continuity between education, coaching, and shopping.
- Treat privacy, accessibility, and honest system states as core brand behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, useful alternative text, and responsive layouts. The experience should welcome beginners and avoid assumptions about identity, role, ability, or prior knowledge.
